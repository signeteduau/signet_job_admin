1. About this Privacy Policy

JLMG PTY LTD, ABN 94 684 960 521, trading as Signet Employment Hub, respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, hold, use, disclose, store and protect personal information when you access or use the Signet Employment Hub mobile application, the application identified as SEH or Signet Jobs, the Signet Employment Hub website and any related candidate, employer, recruitment, communication, notification or support services (collectively, the Services).

Signet Employment Hub provides an employment platform through which candidates may create profiles, search and save jobs, upload resumes, apply for positions, receive recommendations and communicate with employers. Employers may create company profiles, post jobs, review applications, update application status, arrange interviews and communicate with candidates.

This Policy describes the Services and data practices operating at the date shown above. Where we introduce a feature that materially changes the information we collect, use or disclose, we will update this Policy and provide any additional notice or consent required by law.

3. Australian Privacy Framework

We manage personal information in accordance with applicable Australian privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where applicable, the Australian Privacy Principles. These principles address transparent privacy management, collection, notification, use and disclosure, direct marketing, overseas disclosure, information security, access and correction.

The application of particular statutory obligations may depend on the circumstances and legal status of the organisation. Irrespective of that position, we aim to maintain reasonable, transparent and accountable information-handling practices.

4. Scope

This Policy applies to personal information handled through the Services about:

  • job seekers and candidates
  • employers, recruiters and hiring representatives
  • company representatives
  • website and app users

people who contact us, submit feedback or request support; and

other individuals whose information is lawfully provided to us.

This Policy does not govern the independent privacy practices of an employer, recruiter, website, platform or service that is not controlled by us. When a candidate applies for a job, the receiving employer may handle the candidate’s information for its own recruitment purposes and under its own privacy policy and legal obligations.

5. Key Definitions

Account: a registered candidate or employer account within the Services.

Candidate: an individual using the Services to search for or apply for employment opportunities.

Employer: an organisation or authorised representative using the Services to advertise jobs or recruit candidates.

Personal information: information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether or not the information is true or recorded in material form.

Sensitive information: a category of personal information that may receive additional protection under applicable law, such as health, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, criminal record or certain professional association information.

User: a person or organisation that accesses or uses the Services.

We collect personal information when you register, complete or update a profile, upload a photo, company logo, resume or job attachment, create a job advertisement, apply for or save a job, send an in-app message, arrange an interview, provide feedback, contact support or otherwise use an interactive feature of the Services.

6.2 Information generated through your use

Information may be created or recorded through job applications, application status updates, applicant management, interview scheduling, chat messages, notifications, job recommendations, saved jobs, account activity and security events.

6.3 Third-party sign-in

Where enabled, you may choose to access the Services through Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple or Facebook Login. We may receive the basic account information that the relevant provider makes available to us in accordance with your settings and authorisation.

6.4 Device permissions

With your permission, we may access device location, camera, photos, files, notifications and biometric authentication features. You may manage most permissions through your device settings. Refusing or withdrawing a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

13. Candidate Visibility and Resume Disclosure

The current Services do not provide employers with a general talent-search function that allows them to browse all candidate profiles or a complete candidate-resume database. Employers ordinarily receive candidate information when the candidate applies for a job managed by that employer.

When you submit an application:

  • the employer receives the application and resume
  • relevant profile and contact information may be included

the employer may review the application, record notes and update its status; and

the employer may contact you through in-app chat or using contact information you provided.

You should avoid including unnecessary sensitive, financial, identity or unrelated personal information in your resume or profile.

14. In-App Communications

Candidates and employers may communicate through in-app chat, generally in connection with a job application or applicant-management activity.

We may store messages to deliver the chat service, display conversation history, support recruitment communication, investigate complaints or misuse, protect users and comply with legal obligations. Users should not send financial information, identity documents or other highly sensitive information through chat unless a feature specifically requests it and provides an appropriate collection notice.

We may access or disclose messages where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, harassment, unlawful conduct, safety concerns, technical faults or a breach of our terms.

15. Job Recommendations and Automated Features

The Services may recommend jobs using automated, on-device scoring based on factors such as skills, occupation and location. This matching is intended to help users discover potentially relevant opportunities.

The current matching feature is not third-party generative artificial intelligence, does not make employment decisions on behalf of an employer and does not guarantee suitability, eligibility, an interview or employment. Employers remain responsible for recruitment and hiring decisions.

The current app does not include AI resume-writing or AI content-generation tools. Any future material change to automated features or the use of personal information will be reflected in an updated version of this Policy.

16. Notifications and Electronic Communications

We may send service-related communications about account creation and verification, passwords and security, applications, job advertisements, interviews, new jobs, chat messages, platform changes, support requests and other operational matters.

The current application does not implement marketing email or SMS campaigns, apart from authentication or verification messages associated with account services. If direct marketing is introduced, we will provide any notice, consent and unsubscribe mechanism required by law.

You can disable push notifications through your device settings, although doing so may prevent you from receiving timely application, chat or account updates.

17.3 Authentication providers

17.4 Legal and regulatory purposes

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, a court order, warrant or lawful government request; respond to a regulator; investigate suspected unlawful conduct; prevent fraud or security threats; protect users or other persons; or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

17.5 Business restructuring

Personal information may be disclosed as part of a proposed or completed merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale or transfer of all or part of the business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and privacy safeguards.

17.6 With your direction or consent

We may disclose information for another purpose where you direct or authorise us to do so.

We do not sell personal information for payment. If our practices change in a way that constitutes a sale or a materially different form of sharing, we will provide notice and any control required by applicable law before the change takes effect.

21. Overseas Processing and Data Location

Personal information is hosted and processed through Google Firebase services, including Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging and Cloud Functions. The Firebase hosting region for the Services is the United States. Accordingly, personal information handled through these services may be stored or processed in the United States.

Information may also be processed outside Australia where a user selects a third-party sign-in provider or where an employer accesses an application from another country.

Where overseas processing occurs, we take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances to use reputable service providers and protect personal information. Privacy laws in the United States and other countries may differ from Australian privacy laws.

22. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps designed to protect personal information against loss, misuse, interference, unauthorised access, unauthorised disclosure, alteration and destruction. Measures may include authenticated accounts, access controls, encrypted network communications, secure third-party hosting, device-level biometric protection where enabled, restricted administrative access, confidentiality requirements, maintenance and incident-response procedures.

No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials, device and access to your email or third-party sign-in account. Contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

Resume and application files should be protected through authenticated, role-based storage controls so that access is limited to the relevant candidate, authorised Signet Employment Hub personnel and the employer receiving the application.

23. Data Breaches

We will investigate suspected loss, unauthorised access or unauthorised disclosure of personal information. Our response may include containing the incident, assessing the affected information and likely harm, reducing ongoing risk, improving controls and notifying affected people or regulators where required by law.

Where the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme applies and an eligible data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will take steps required under the Privacy Act, which may include notification to affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

24. Data Retention

We retain personal information while an account remains active and for as long as it is needed to provide the Services requested by the user.

When a user completes account deletion, the associated account information under our control is deleted at that time. The post-deletion retention period is 0 days. Signet Employment Hub does not maintain a separate custom database or intentionally keep a duplicate copy of deleted account data in another location.

Information that is no longer required for an active account or the delivery of the Services is deleted in accordance with the account-deletion process described below.

25. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Signet Employment Hub account and associated personal information.

25.1 Requesting deletion

You may initiate deletion through the account settings in the app. If you cannot access the app, you may submit a deletion request through https://signetemploymenthub.com/delete-account or email privacy@jobportal.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”.

25.2 Verification and processing

We may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the requester and confirm that deletion is intentional. Once the deletion process is confirmed, the account and associated personal information under our control are deleted immediately.

25.3 Information covered

Account deletion covers profile information, profile images, resumes, saved jobs, job applications, chat records linked to the account, feedback, notification tokens and authentication records under our control in Firebase.

25.4 Post-deletion retention

The operational post-deletion retention period is 0 days. We do not keep deleted account data in a separate custom database.

Deletion of a Signet Employment Hub account does not automatically delete copies of an application or resume already downloaded or independently retained by an employer. The employer is responsible for handling those copies under its own legal obligations and privacy practices.

26. Access and Correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, incomplete, out of date, irrelevant or misleading. Some information can be reviewed or updated directly through your account.

Requests may be sent to privacy@jobportal.com with the subject “Privacy Access Request” or “Privacy Correction Request”. We may ask you to verify your identity. We will respond within a reasonable period and may refuse access where permitted or required by law. Where appropriate, we will explain the reason for refusal and any available complaint options.

27. Consent and Preferences

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it by changing app permissions or device settings, disabling notifications, removing optional profile information, contacting us or deleting your account. Withdrawal does not invalidate handling that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

Some information is necessary to provide core Services. If you do not provide required information or withdraw a necessary permission, the relevant function may not be available.

28. Anonymity and Pseudonymity

You may browse publicly available information without identifying yourself where the Services permit it. An account and identifying information may be required where it is impracticable to provide a feature anonymously, including creating a candidate or employer profile, posting a job, submitting an application, uploading a resume, communicating with another user or managing application status.

29. Accuracy of Information

You should provide accurate, complete and current information and update your profile when relevant details change. Employers are responsible for ensuring that company details, job advertisements, salary information and recruitment information they provide are accurate, lawful and not misleading.

30. Children and Young People

We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child contrary to applicable law. If we become aware that information has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or obtain any authorisation required by law.

We do not guarantee that every employer, job advertisement or external website follows the same privacy practices as the Services.
